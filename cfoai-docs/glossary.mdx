Get familiar with Runway's structure and the terms you'll see throughout the product.

## Terms

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Table blocks" icon="table-cells">
    A mini spreadsheet built into CFO.ai — each report or forecast you build lives in its own table block.

    <Frame>
      ![Profit & Loss table block in CFO.ai showing monthly revenue, expenses, and margin rows](/images/cfoai-docs/table-blocks.png)
    </Frame>
  </Card>

  <Card title="Integrations" icon="link">
    The connections that pull your data in automatically — QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, Sheets, and CSV files.

    <Frame>
      ![CFO.ai integrations panel showing CSV upload, Google Sheets, NetSuite, QuickBooks Online, and Xero](/images/cfoai-docs/integrations.png)
    </Frame>
  </Card>

  <Card title="Scenarios" icon="code-branch">
    Different "what if" versions of your plan, so you can compare outcomes before deciding what to do.

    <Frame>
      ![Scenario picker in CFO.ai showing Main Scenario, Best Case, and Budget compared side by side on a P&L](/images/cfoai-docs/scenarios.png)
    </Frame>
  </Card>

  <Card title="Page" icon="file-lines">
    A single screen inside CFO.ai focused on one topic — like a folder or tab in your workspace.

    <Frame>
      ![CFO.ai sidebar showing pages like Profit & Loss, Default Page, and Integration import next to the Profit & Loss workspace](/images/cfoai-docs/page.png)
    </Frame>
  </Card>

  <Card title="Database" icon="database">
    An organized list of similar items, like contacts or expenses, made up of rows and columns.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Metrics" icon="chart-simple">
    The key numbers you track to understand the business — cash, burn, revenue, and more.

    <Frame>
      ![P&L snippet showing key metrics: Total revenue, Total cost of revenue, Gross profit, and Gross margin %](/images/cfoai-docs/metrics.png)
    </Frame>
  </Card>

  <Card title="Rows" icon="bars">
    A single line of data in a table block, like an expense category, vendor, or line item.

    <Frame>
      ![P&L table showing individual rows like Marketing & advertising, Rent & lease costs, and Travel & meals](/images/cfoai-docs/rows.png)
    </Frame>
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
